# LinkedIn Post: My Wellness Threads

A couple of years ago, I stopped fighting my body and started listening to it.

For years, "wellness" lived in a tab I'd promise to open later. Then later became now. The aches. The energy dips. The slow creep on the scale. The little signals the body sends when it's been ignored too long. They all started talking at once.

So I picked up a framework from my wellness guru, Luke Coutinho. Nothing fancy. Nothing loud. Just four threads I try to weave through every ordinary day. I call them My Wellness Threads, and they live at pbdesk.com.

Here's the gist of each one.

🥗 Thread 01 — Cellular Nutrition
Luke flipped the question for me. Instead of "what should I eat?", he asks, "what do your 37 trillion cells actually need?" That single shift changed my grocery list. Whole, natural, unprocessed foods. More plants. Real fats. Fewer things in shiny packets. Food as fuel for immunity and repair, not just calories. The grocery list gets simpler. The body gets quieter.

🚶 Thread 02 — Adequate Exercise
No gym. No 5 AM heroics. Just movement that fits a meeting-heavy life. 10K steps. Short home workouts. Stretching between Zoom calls. A little strength work, because I'm not training for a marathon. I'm training for my 70s. The science is boring and beautiful: regular movement boosts endorphins, sharpens focus, and protects the body you have to live in for the long haul. Consistency beats intensity. Every time.

😴 Thread 03 — Quality Sleep
This is the hardest one for me. Honestly. I used to treat sleep as the thing I cut when there wasn't enough day. Luke calls sleep the one thing the body can't outsource. He's right. Now I treat it like a meeting I can't reschedule. Quality over quantity. Same wind-down ritual. Phone parked outside the bedroom. Restorative sleep is when the body repairs, hormones balance, and immunity rebuilds. Skip it, and nothing else in the framework works.

❤️ Thread 04 — Emotional Wellness
The one I used to skip. The one that turned out to be load-bearing. Managing stress. Letting go of the stuff that doesn't belong to me. Cultivating mindfulness and gratitude. A weekly emotional detox is just as important as a clean diet. In a noisy, always-on world, this is the thread that prevents burnout from becoming a permanent address.

Four threads. One desk. One life.

They don't work alone. Bad sleep wrecks nutrition choices. Skipped movement spikes anxiety. Unmanaged emotions destroy sleep. Pull one, the others wobble. Weave all four, and life feels steadier.

I'm not a doctor. I'm not selling a program. I'm just someone taking notes from a teacher who's been quietly right for years.

If any of this resonates, I'm sharing the journey at pbdesk.com/bites. Come read, push back, share what's working for you.

Thank you, Luke Coutinho, for teaching with patience.

After all, health is wealth. 💙

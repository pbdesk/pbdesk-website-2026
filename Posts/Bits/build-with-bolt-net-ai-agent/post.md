# Bolt.new: Build Full-Stack Apps by Describing Them

Bolt.new is a browser-based AI app builder from StackBlitz. The idea is simple: describe what you want, and it builds a working web app for you.

You're not writing code from scratch. You're having a conversation with an AI that writes the frontend, connects a backend, and sets up the database. The result is a live, deployable application.

## What it actually does

When you type something like "build a recipe tracker where users can save and tag their favorite meals," Bolt.new generates the UI, the data models, and the logic to tie them together. It uses React and Vite under the hood, but you never need to think about that unless you want to.

You make changes by describing them. "Add a search bar" or "make the button blue" are valid instructions. If the output isn't quite right, you iterate with words, not code.

## Who this is for

Bolt.new works well for people who have an app idea but not the engineering background to build it from scratch. Designers testing a concept. Product managers prototyping a feature. Indie hackers who want to ship something without hiring a developer.

It's also useful for developers who want to scaffold something fast and then customize the output.

## The catch

Complex apps still need human judgment. If your requirements are specific or the logic gets involved, you'll hit limits. But for MVPs, internal tools, and quick demos, Bolt.new gets you surprisingly far.

The bar to building something real just dropped. If you have an idea, it's worth spending an afternoon with Bolt.new to see how far you can get.
